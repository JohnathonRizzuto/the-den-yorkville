About The Den

Yorkville's Spot for a Good Time

No pretense. No dress code. Just a place where you grab a cold one, order something off the grill, and catch live music on the weekends. The Den sits right on Heustis in downtown Yorkville -- the kind of neighborhood bar every town needs.

Formerly known as Dakotas, the space got a fresh start with the same laid-back energy. The vibe is mellow, the food hits, and the staff actually pays attention. Walk in a stranger, leave a regular.
